Wildlife Shorts

A private desktop tool that the getpsyched! YouTube channel (@getpsychedmix1) uses to publish its own short animal videos.

What the app does

  • Builds short vertical videos on the channel owner's own computer, from licensed stock footage and original music, sound effects, captions and synthetic narration.
  • Uploads each finished video to the getpsyched! channel as private, sets its title, description and custom thumbnail, and schedules the publish time, using the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads back the video status to confirm the upload and the schedule, and records the result in local logs.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the getpsyched! channel. The app is not offered to the public, has no user accounts and no sign-up. It runs as a command-line program on one personal computer and refuses to work with any other channel.

Google sign-in and permissions

The channel owner signs in once with Google (OAuth 2.0 for desktop apps) and grants two YouTube scopes:

  • youtube.upload: upload videos and set thumbnails;
  • youtube.force-ssl: update the metadata and schedule of the channel's own videos and read the channel and video status.

The app never deletes videos and never acts on other channels. Details of what is stored and shared are in the Privacy Policy.

Wildlife Shorts uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
